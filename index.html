Kaifeng Lin （林凯风）

kflin@stu.pku.edu.cn

I am a PhD candidate at Peking University, advised by Prof. Kaihui Liu. My research focuses on nonlinear and quantum optics in two-dimensional materials. I study how the structure of these materials shapes their interaction with light and how their optical properties can be used to generate and control light in integrated photonic devices.
