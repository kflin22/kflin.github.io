I am a PhD candidate at Peking University, advised by Prof. <a class="advisor-link" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lmw5JTMAAAAJ&amp;hl=zh-CN&amp;oi=ao">Kaihui Liu</a>. My research focuses on nonlinear and quantum optics in two-dimensional materials. I study how the structure of these materials shapes their interaction with light and how their optical properties can be used to generate and control light in integrated photonic devices.
